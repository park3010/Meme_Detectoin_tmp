\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Task and Framework Overview}
\label{sec:method:overview}

Given a meme $\mathcal{M}=(I,T)$ consisting of an image $I$ and OCR
text $T$, we formulate harmful meme understanding as structured
interpretation rather than binary classification alone.
The model predicts

\begin{equation}
\hat{\mathcal{Y}}
=
\left(
\hat{y}^{h},
\hat{y}^{p},
\hat{y}^{g},
\hat{y}^{i},
\hat{\mathbf{y}}^{r},
\hat{y}^{m}
\right),
\label{eq:structured_output}
\end{equation}

corresponding to harmfulness, target presence, target granularity,
primary intent, multi-label rhetorical tactics, and multimodal
relation, respectively.

Figure~\ref{fig:framework_overview} illustrates the overall framework.
Given a meme image and its text, the model constructs complementary internal
evidence and external knowledge candidates. The central evidence-aware verifier
determines which external candidates are sufficiently relevant, valid, and
task-supportive to influence downstream prediction; only verified evidence is
passed to task-aware fusion, while verification decisions are retained for
provenance.

\subsection{Internal and External Evidence}
\label{sec:method:evidence}

Stage~A extracts complementary internal evidence from global image
content, OCR text, localized visual cues, and image--text interaction:

\begin{equation}
\mathcal{E}^{\mathrm{int}}
=
f_A(I,T).
\end{equation}

These observations provide the meme-conditioned context used both
for prediction and for judging whether external information is
actually relevant.

When the meme may require additional social, cultural, or
event-specific context, Stage~B constructs evidence-conditioned
queries and obtains a candidate knowledge set

\begin{equation}
\mathcal{K}^{\mathrm{cand}}
=
f_B(\mathcal{E}^{\mathrm{int}}).
\end{equation}

The paper-eligible retrieval resource is constructed only from the
HarMeme training partition.
Candidates may originate from retrieval or context-generation
mechanisms, but their origin does not imply evidential validity.
Implementation details of the evidence extractors, query construction,
and hybrid retrieval are provided in the supplementary material.


\subsection{Evidence-Aware Knowledge Verification}
\label{sec:method:verifier}

External context can be topically related to a meme while still being
irrelevant to its harmful interpretation.
Moreover, evidence useful for identifying a target may not support the
same conclusion about intent or rhetorical strategy.
We therefore introduce an explicit verifier between acquisition and
fusion.

Let $\mathbf{q}_{\mathrm{int}}$ summarize the internal evidence and
$\mathbf{k}_j$ denote an external candidate.
The relevance scorer uses their pairwise interaction:

\begin{equation}
\mathbf{x}_j
=
\left[
\mathbf{q}_{\mathrm{int}};
\mathbf{k}_j;
\mathbf{q}_{\mathrm{int}}\odot\mathbf{k}_j;
\left|\mathbf{q}_{\mathrm{int}}-\mathbf{k}_j\right|
\right],
\label{eq:verifier_features}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
s_j^{\mathrm{rel}}
=
\sigma\!\left(
\operatorname{MLP}_{\mathrm{rel}}(\mathbf{x}_j)
\right).
\label{eq:verifier_relevance}
\end{equation}

Relevance alone is insufficient.
For each candidate, the verifier additionally records
(i) validity-related cues and
(ii) task-specific support for target, intent, and rhetorical-tactic
interpretation.
Conceptually, each candidate is associated with

\begin{equation}
\mathbf{s}_j
=
\left[
s_j^{\mathrm{rel}},
s_j^{\mathrm{val}},
\mathbf{s}_j^{\mathrm{sup}}
\right],
\end{equation}

where $\mathbf{s}_j^{\mathrm{sup}}$ contains the task-specific support
signals.
A fixed verification policy then constructs

\begin{equation}
\mathcal{K}^{\mathrm{ver}}
=
\mathcal{V}
\left(
\mathcal{E}^{\mathrm{int}},
\mathcal{K}^{\mathrm{cand}}
\right)
\subseteq
\mathcal{K}^{\mathrm{cand}}.
\label{eq:verified_bank}
\end{equation}

Importantly, rejected candidates are not discarded from the audit
record.
Their origin, relevance, validity, support state, and final
accepted/rejected status are preserved.
This enables us to distinguish retrieval quality from verification
behavior in the subsequent analysis.


\subsection{Verified Evidence Fusion and Prediction}
\label{sec:method:fusion}

Verified knowledge is fused with internal meme evidence using
task-aware reasoning.
For each prediction task $q$,

\begin{equation}
\mathbf{z}^{(q)}
=
\operatorname{Fuse}_{q}
\left(
\mathcal{E}^{\mathrm{int}},
\mathcal{K}^{\mathrm{ver}}
\right),
\qquad
\hat{\mathbf{y}}^{(q)}
=
\operatorname{Head}_{q}
\left(
\mathbf{z}^{(q)}
\right).
\label{eq:task_fusion}
\end{equation}

If no external candidate passes verification, the model falls back to
internal evidence.
The rhetorical-tactic head produces multi-label logits, whereas the
remaining five outputs are single-label predictions.

We train the six tasks jointly with a masked multi-task objective,

\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}
=
\frac{1}{N}
\sum_{n=1}^{N}
w_n
\sum_{q}
\lambda_q m_n^q
\ell_q
\left(
\hat{\mathbf{y}}_n^{(q)},y_n^{(q)}
\right),
\label{eq:multitask_objective}
\end{equation}

where $m_n^q$ excludes unavailable or invalid field-level supervision.
The model additionally serializes selected internal and verified
external evidence together with provenance and a concise rationale.
These records support auditing and analysis, but we do not interpret
them as causal explanations.
