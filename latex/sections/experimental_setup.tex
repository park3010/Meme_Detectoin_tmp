\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\subsection{Protocol and Compared Methods}
\label{sec:setup:protocol}

We study cross-domain generalization by training and validating
trainable methods only on HarMeme~\cite{pramanick2021harmeme,pramanick2021momenta}
and evaluating them on the 9,000-example Facebook Hateful Memes benchmark
(FHM)~\cite{kiela2020hateful}.
FHM is used exclusively as the held-out target set and is never used
for model selection, threshold tuning, prompt development, or
retrieval construction.
Retrieval-based conditions use only knowledge resources constructed
from the HarMeme training partition.
Detailed preprocessing, duplicate/conflict handling, source splitting,
annotation auditing, and leakage checks are provided in the
supplementary material.

Harmfulness uses the original binary benchmark labels.
For structured evaluation, we additionally assess target presence and
granularity, primary intent, rhetorical tactic, and multimodal relation
using schema-validated FHM silver annotations.
Structured results are computed only over samples with valid
field-level supervision, and their coverage is reported explicitly.

We compare against text-only, image-only, and simple multimodal
controls; specialized harmful-meme methods including
HateCLIPper~\cite{kumar2022hateclipper},
MemeCLIP~\cite{shah2024memeclip}, RGCL~\cite{mei2024rgcl}, and
RA-HMD~\cite{mei2025rahmd};
and Qwen2.5-VL-7B~\cite{bai2025qwen25vl} under
\textsc{Direct}, \textsc{Rationale}, and train-only \textsc{RAG}
inference.
The complete framework is further compared with four controlled
ablations: \textsc{w/o Retrieval}, \textsc{w/o Support Verifier},
\textsc{w/o Task-Aware Gate}, and
\textsc{w/o Structured Auxiliary}.
Full reproduction details for external baselines are provided in the
supplementary material.

\subsection{Implementation and Metrics}
\label{sec:setup:metrics}

Our framework uses frozen OpenCLIP ViT-B/32 and DeBERTa-v3-base
backbones~\cite{radford2021clip,cherti2023openclip,he2023debertav3}
with trainable projection, reasoning, and structured
prediction modules.
All model and decoding decisions are selected using HarMeme
validation only.
For multi-label rhetorical tactics, the global sigmoid threshold is
selected on the source validation set and then frozen for FHM.

Macro-F1 is the primary harmfulness metric, with AUROC reported as a
complementary ranking metric.
Structured single-label fields are evaluated using Macro-F1 over valid
silver annotations, while rhetorical tactics additionally use
multi-label Micro-F1.
Complete trainable seed sets are reported as mean~$\pm$~standard
deviation; deterministic VLM conditions and canonical-seed ablations
are reported as single runs.

Our model and built-in controls are executed on RTX~3090 GPUs, whereas
external reproduced models are executed on a separate RTX~5090
comparison server.
We therefore do not interpret cross-system wall-clock measurements as
direct speed comparisons.
Hyperparameters, hardware details, prompts, retrieval configuration,
runtime statistics, and reproducibility manifests are reported in the
supplementary material.
