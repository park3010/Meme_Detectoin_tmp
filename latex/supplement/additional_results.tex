\section{Additional Results and Diagnostics}
\label{sec:supp:additional-results}

\textbf{Result placeholder.}  The final audited export will add per-seed
results, rhetorical-tactic Micro-F1/per-label F1/exact match, verifier
candidate-flow summaries, knowledge-source diagnostics, runtime and memory
measurements, and prespecified significance tests.  No value is inserted
until its run is complete, its strict artifact/leakage audit passes, and its
suite and metric are paper eligible.

For verifier-flow analysis, the planned primary quantities are acquired,
retained, and rejected candidates per meme and the resulting acceptance rate.
Origin and task-support strata will be computed post hoc from serialized
provenance without changing retrieval or verification.  Evidence and
rationale diagnostics remain descriptive and are not treated as human
judgments or causal-faithfulness measurements.
