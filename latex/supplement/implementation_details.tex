\section{Model and Training Details}
\label{sec:supp:implementation}

Stage A encodes global image content with a locally stored OpenCLIP
ViT-B/32 checkpoint~\cite{radford2021clip,cherti2023openclip} and OCR text
with a locally stored DeBERTa-v3-base model~\cite{he2023debertav3}.  The
pretrained backbones are frozen, whereas their newly materialized projections,
the cross-modal incongruity path, evidence aggregation, reasoning layers, and
formal output heads are trainable.  Projection modules are materialized before
optimizer construction.  Text is truncated to 64 tokens, at most three local
regions are retained, and all task-facing states use width 256.

Stage B forms at most six queries and retrieves at most eight external
candidates.  Stage C scores relevance with a trainable MLP and combines it
with fixed validity and task-support signals under the frozen verification
policy.  Stage D uses four attention heads and two reasoning layers; its
task-aware gate produces target-, intent-, and tactic-conditioned latents.
Stage E exposes six formal trainable-logit heads.  Rationale and evidence
renderings are serialized for audit but are not substituted for these logits
in formal metrics.

The canonical framework uses AdamW with learning rate $10^{-4}$ for five
epochs and patience two, selecting checkpoints on HarMeme validation only.
The implementation consumes one meme per optimization step.  Confidence-based
sample weights are read from normalized supervision, and uncertain examples
may be downweighted without changing their class label.  Model seeds are
42, 52, 123, 777, and 2026 for complete trainable comparisons; canonical
component analysis uses seed 42.  Pretrained weights and tokenizers are loaded
locally.  The slow SentencePiece DeBERTa tokenizer is frozen under the
documented compatibility-warning policy; no regex compatibility option is
silently enabled.
