\section{Dataset Preparation and Split Integrity}
\label{sec:supp:data}

The frozen protocol is \texttt{harmeme\_to\_fhm\_v2}.  HarMeme combines the
COVID-19 Harm-C collection~\cite{pramanick2021harmeme} and its U.S.-politics
Harm-P extension~\cite{pramanick2021momenta}.  The source manifest contains
7,013 raw records.  A source-only integrity audit excludes 72 redundant or
conflicting records and retains 6,941 records for supervised development.
The immutable group-aware split then assigns 5,552 records to training and
1,389 to validation.  Split seed 42 is the fixed \emph{data-split seed}; it
is independent of the five model-initialization seeds used for multi-seed
experiments.

Duplicate resolution operates on canonical \nolinkurl{dataset::sample_id}
keys and decoded RGB-pixel hashes.  Every decoded-image group is an
indivisible split unit.  Both members of a raw-source harmfulness conflict
are excluded; members of a valid structured-label conflict are likewise
excluded.  For exact same-label or format-only duplicates, one
lexicographically selected canonical record is retained.  Source-confirmed
records that share an image but carry different OCR text are retained as
distinct multimodal samples, but the complete image group is assigned to one
partition.  Consequently, both decoded-image and exact image--OCR overlap
between source train and validation are zero.  Raw images and annotations
remain unchanged; resolution is expressed only through versioned manifests.

The Facebook Hateful Memes benchmark (FHM)~\cite{kiela2020hateful} is an
immutable 9,000-record target test set.  It is excluded from training,
validation, checkpoint selection, threshold selection, calibration,
hyperparameter search, prompt development, few-shot examples, retrieval
construction, and knowledge-source selection.  Memotion is disabled by the
active protocol.  Dataset manifests are addressed by content and manifest
hashes (Table~\ref{tab:supp:hashes}), and strict preflight rejects a paper run
that selects a legacy split, mixes protocol versions, or activates a
forbidden dataset.
