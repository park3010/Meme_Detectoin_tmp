\section{Controlled Ablation Definitions}
\label{sec:supp:ablations}

All canonical component analyses use model seed 42 and apply the same
ablation during source training, source validation, and held-out inference.
Unless stated below, the source split, model dimensions, optimizer, learning
rate, task weights, checkpoint rule, and decoding policy are unchanged.

\begin{description}
  \item[\textsc{w/o Retrieval}.]
  Stage B retrieval and context generation return zero candidates; Stage C
  therefore returns an empty verified bank and Stage D uses internal evidence
  only.  All six formal logits losses remain active.

  \item[\textsc{w/o Support Verifier}.]
  Retrieval, learned relevance, and validity remain active.  Support columns
  for target, intent, and tactic are neutralized to the insufficient state, the
  support bonus is zero, and candidate scores reduce to relevance times
  validity.  All six formal logits losses remain active.

  \item[\textsc{w/o Task-Aware Gate}.]
  The three task-routing gates are replaced by their shared mean scalar.  The
  differentiable fused/shared reasoning path and task-specific latents remain;
  only task-specific gate variation is removed.

  \item[\textsc{w/o Structured Auxiliary}.]
  Two auxiliary losses are excluded from the joint objective: target presence
  and multimodal relation.  Harmfulness, target-granularity, primary-intent, and
  rhetorical-tactic logits losses remain active, and output serialization is
  unchanged.
\end{description}
