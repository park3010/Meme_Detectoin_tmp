\section{Retrieval Resource and Leakage Control}
\label{sec:supp:retrieval}

The paper-eligible retrieval profile is
\texttt{harmeme\_train\_v2}.  It was built offline by replaying auditable
local query caches for the v2 HarMeme training partition.  All 5,552 training
samples occur as query origins (100\% coverage), producing a frozen corpus of
4,784 unique documents.  Query identifiers hash the dataset name, sample ID,
and exact cached query.  The corpus manifest reports zero origins from FHM,
HarMeme validation, Memotion, or unknown sources, and records that network
access was not used during construction.

Hybrid lookup combines a sparse BM25/token-statistics index with a
deterministic 256-dimensional hashed-vector index.  The latter is a fixed
lexical hashing representation, \emph{not} a pretrained semantic embedding
model.  Its purpose is reproducible dense-vector lookup over the frozen
corpus.  Corpus, document-provenance, sparse-index, and dense-index checksums
are bound to the v2 source-split manifest.  Paper preflight rejects a
retrieval profile whose source split hash differs from the active protocol.
Offline corpus and index construction are reported separately from online
retrieval and inference costs.
