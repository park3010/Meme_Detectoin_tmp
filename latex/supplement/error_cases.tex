\section{Qualitative Error Examples}
\label{sec:supp:error-cases}

\textbf{Result placeholder.}  After the final held-out export is frozen,
representative cases may be organized by the prespecified observable
categories: insufficient contextual knowledge, target confusion, cultural or
event-specific ambiguity, image--text incongruity, rhetorical-tactic
confusion, and irrelevant or insufficiently discriminative retrieved context.
Each case will use only the prediction, silver structured annotation, and
serialized evidence record.  The categories are descriptive, not causal, and
will not be used to revise the model, retrieval configuration, or decoding
policy.  No claim of human adjudication will be made.
